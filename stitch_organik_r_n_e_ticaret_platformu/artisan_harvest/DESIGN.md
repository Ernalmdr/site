## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an earthy, high-end agricultural direct-to-consumer (D2C) commerce experience. The visual language balances agrarian authenticity with contemporary luxury editorial aesthetics. The target audience values traceability, single-origin natural nutrition, eco-conscious packaging, and a sensory culinary experience. 

The aesthetic merges **Tactile Organic Warmth** with **Modern Editorial Minimalism**. Interfaces eschew sterile corporate whiteboards in favor of comforting, warm milk-toned canvases, deep roasted nut pigments, and botanical canopy greens. Micro-details emulate physical paper stock, debossed botanical seals, and soft organic framing without falling into outdated skeuomorphic kitsch. The interface feels grounded, fragrant, honest, and distinctly premium.

## Colors

The palette derives strictly from the life cycle of the orchard: deep loam soil, sun-ripened leaves, roasted kernels, and untamed flora.

- **Primary (`#6F4E37` - Roasted Hazelnut):** Represents primary brand identity, active header hierarchies, key emphasis badges, and anchoring surfaces. Accent variant `#8B5A2B` (Warm Kernel) is used for secondary focus and hover states on primary elements.
- **Secondary (`#2E5A36` - Grove Olive):** Conveys freshness, agricultural certification, bio-origin badges, sustainability tags, and trusted success states. Extended with `#4C7C54` for leaf-toned accents and interactive highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Honey Amber):** Provides the vibrant, warm commerce accent necessary for conversion elements: promotion badges, cart notification pings, stock urgency indicators, and star rating highlights.
- **Neutral Core (`#261C14` - Dark Soil):** High-contrast, warm charcoal-brown serving as the primary text color to avoid the clinical harshness of `#000000`.
- **Canvas & Surface Tier:**
  - Base Background: `#FDFBF7` (Hazelnut Milk / Raw Cream).
  - Raised Surface / Card Background: `#FFFFFF`.
  - Secondary Surface / Nested Wells: `#F5EFEB` (Milled Flour / Raw Parchment).
  - Subtle Dividing Lines: `#E8DFD5` (Warm Flax Border).

## Typography

The pairing creates an organic editorial dialogue:
- **Display & Headings (Epilogue):** An expressive, structured sans-serif with architectural curves and craft personality. It imparts artisanal confidence in packaging titles, story banners, and category hero blocks.
- **Body, Navigation & Data (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A friendly, geometric humanist typeface optimized for high-density e-commerce flows (ingredient tables, nutrient percentages, checkout labels, pricing strings).

**Stylistic Hierarchy:**
- Numerical prices should consistently render in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold/bold weights to guarantee tabular clarity.
- Currency symbols use `label-md` baseline offsets.
- Product provenance tags and certifications leverage uppercase `label-tag` styling with slightly expanded kerning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy relies on an airy, breathable **fixed-max fluid hybrid grid**:
- Maximum content width: `1320px` centered on wide desktop screens.
- Desktop (1024px+): 12-column grid, `gutter: 1.5rem`, outer canvas padding `margin: 3rem`.
- Tablet (768px - 1023px): 8-column grid, `gutter: 1.25rem`, outer canvas padding `margin: 2rem`.
- Mobile (&lt;768px): 4-column grid, `gutter-sm: 1rem`, outer margin `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to multiples of 8px (0.5rem) and 4px (0.25rem). Dense, cluttered marketplace visuals are prohibited; editorial white space is celebrated as an indicator of premium organic freshness.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects sharp artificial synthetic dropshadows. Depth is established through **tonal layering** and **warm ambient umber shadows**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** Tone `#FDFBF7`. Completely matte.
- **Layer 1 (Cards, Product Tiles):** Solid `#FFFFFF` fill resting on subtle warm perimeter boundary `1px solid #E8DFD5` paired with an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(111, 78, 55, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Dropdowns, Quick-view Drawers, Sticky ATC Bar):** Floating cards with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(70, 48, 32, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Cart Slide-overs):** `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(38, 28, 20, 0.16)` backdrop overlay filled with warm tint `rgba(38, 28, 20, 0.45)` with a backdrop blur of `4px`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts smooth, organic geometry that evokes natural seeds, river stones, and curved packaging bottles:
- Standard interactive elements (inputs, standard buttons, selection pills) use a base radius of `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- Product cards, category tiles, and structural panels apply `1rem` (`16px`).
- Feature banners, hero containers, and promotional trays utilize `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- Natural pill treatments (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) are reserved exclusively for certification seals ("%100 Organik", "Giresun Kalite"), quantity steppers, and floating price stickers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Buy Now):** Background `#6F4E37`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.5rem`. Hover shifts to `#8B5A2B` with a soft scale lift (`translate-y: -1px`). Focus outline is a 2px offset ring in `#2E5A36`.
- **Secondary Action (View Story / Direct Origin):** Transparent surface with a 1.5px border in `#2E5A36`, text `#2E5A36`. Hover fills with `#2E5A36` with text shifting to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost (Favorite, Clear, Share):** Transparent background, text `#6F4E37`. Hover receives `#F5EFEB` warm background tint.

### Product Cards
- Elevated card (`#FFFFFF`) with `1rem` rounded corners and `1px solid #E8DFD5` border.
- Media wrapper features a subtle `1:1` or `4:5` aspect ratio with warm ivory backdrop `#FDFBF7` behind crisp transparent product photography.
- Top overlay handles: Floating pill tags (e.g., `#2E5A36` background with white text for "Yeni Mahsul", or `#D97706` for "Tükenmek Üzere").
- Bottom interaction: Integrated inline quantity incrementer and instant Add-to-Basket icon button.

### Badges & Chips
- **Dietary & Origin Chips (Glütensiz, Çifte Kavrulmuş, Taş Değirmen):** Pill-shaped, background `#F5EFEB`, border `1px solid #E8DFD5`, text `#6F4E37`, size `label-md`. Selected state switches to `#2E5A36` fill with white text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E8DFD5`, text `#261C14`, placeholder text `#8B5A2B` at 50% opacity.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#2E5A36` with an ambient glow of `rgba(46, 90, 54, 0.15)`.
- Weight Selector (e.g., "250g", "500g", "1000g"): Segmented button grouping with smooth radio transition; active item features `#6F4E37` fill and white label.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes: `20px x 20px`, `4px` border radius. Inactive: `1.5px solid #E8DFD5`, active: `#2E5A36` filled with white tick icon.
- Radios: Circular `20px`, active state displays `#2E5A36` inner disc with `3px` white gutter.

### Harvest Traceability Card (Specialized Commerce Component)
- Background `#F5EFEB`, inner border `1px dashed #6F4E37` at 30% opacity.
- Features a rustic mini seal, coordinates of origin (e.g., "Keşap, Giresun - Rakım: 450m"), harvest date, and farmer signature script line.
